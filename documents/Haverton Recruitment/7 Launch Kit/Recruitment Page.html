For care providers

How we work

  • We agree written terms with you before we start.
  • We take a full brief: the role, the service, pay, hours and the checks you need.
  • We screen every candidate by interview and check their work history, references and qualifications before we introduce them.
  • We only send you a CV the candidate has agreed to share for your role.
  • After they start, we check in with you and the new starter at 7, 30, 60 and 90 days.

As the employer, you remain responsible for your own right to work and DBS checks before the person starts.

Roles we recruit

  • Care Assistants and Support Workers
  • Senior Care Assistants and Team Leaders
  • Care Coordinators and Field Care Supervisors
  • Deputy Managers and Registered Managers
  • Quality, compliance and operations roles

Fees

You pay only when a candidate you hired through us starts work. The fee is a percentage of the first-year salary:

RoleFee
Care Assistants and Support Workers12.5%
Senior Care Assistants and Team Leaders15%
Deputy Managers, Care Coordinators, Field Care Supervisors17.5%
Registered Managers, Home Managers, Clinical Leads20%
Senior operations, quality and compliance roles22.5% or agreed fixed fee

Fees exclude VAT [confirm VAT position]. If a new starter leaves early, we offer a free replacement within 30 days, a 50% credit for days 31 to 60 and a 25% credit for days 61 to 90, as set out in our Terms of Business.

For candidates

Our promise to you

  • We never charge you a fee for finding you work.
  • We only send your CV to an employer after you agree to that specific role.
  • We tell you the employer, the pay and the hours before you decide.
  • We keep in touch after you start.

How to register

Email your CV to info@havertoncare.co.uk with the type of role and the areas you can travel to, or call 01322 879778. We will arrange a short conversation.

You must have the right to work in the UK. We do not provide visa sponsorship or immigration advice.

Temporary work: We are not offering temporary shifts yet. To hear when we start, email us with the subject line "Temporary interest" and tell us your role and the areas you can travel to. We will not ask for your documents until we are ready to offer work.

Your information
