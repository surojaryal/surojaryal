Running real payroll

This calculator estimates figures. To actually pay staff and stay legal, you report to HMRC in real time. Here is the short version.

  • Register as an employer with HMRC before the first payday (free, online). You get a PAYE reference.
  • Use HMRC-recognised payroll software to run each pay run and send a Full Payment Submission (RTI) to HMRC on or before payday. Several packages are free for fewer than 10 employees; others cost £5–£15 a month. HMRC's own Basic PAYE Tools is free. A hand-built spreadsheet cannot file RTI.
  • Give every worker a payslip showing gross, deductions and net.
  • Pay HMRC the income tax and NI you deducted, plus your employer NI, monthly (or quarterly if small).
  • Set up a workplace pension (NEST is free) and auto-enrol eligible staff; make the employer contributions.
  • Claim the Employment Allowance if eligible — a tick-box in the software.
  • Keep records for at least 3 years, and run minimum-wage checks that include paid travel and training time.
Founder tip: do the pay runs yourself in free or low-cost recognised software once the pattern is simple, but have a small-practice accountant or payroll bureau set it up and check the first few months. For temporary staffing, where you may be the employer of many short-term workers, a payroll bureau often earns its fee. This tool and page are general guidance, not regulated tax or payroll advice.
