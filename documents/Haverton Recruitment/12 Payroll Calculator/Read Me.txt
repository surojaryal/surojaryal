HAVERTON PAYROLL CALCULATOR AND SETUP GUIDE

This folder contains:
- Haverton Payroll Calculator.html - the offline calculator (below).
- Haverton Payroll Setup Guide.docx - how to set up and run real payroll: registering as an
  employer, software, onboarding, each pay run, paying HMRC, pensions, year-end and
  temporary staffing payroll, with a setup checklist.

Double-click "Haverton Payroll Calculator.html" to open it in your web browser.
It works offline, on a computer or phone. Nothing you type is saved or sent anywhere.

It has four tabs:
1. Pay run calculator - take-home pay for one worker (income tax, National Insurance,
   pension and optional student loan) and what they cost you as the employer.
   Enter an hourly rate or an annual salary; switch between weekly, monthly and yearly.
2. Temp charge & margin - for temporary shifts: what you pay, your on-costs and the
   rate you charge the client, giving your margin per hour.
3. Rates 2026/27 - the minimum wage, tax bands, National Insurance, pension and
   student loan thresholds the calculator uses.
4. How to run payroll - the real steps: register as an employer, use HMRC-recognised
   software to file RTI, payslips, pensions and the Employment Allowance.

It is always available online, too, inside Haverton Operations:
operation.havertoncare.co.uk/payroll/  (or the "Payroll Calculator" link in the CRM sidebar).

IMPORTANT
This is a planning estimator, not a payslip and not regulated advice. It annualises tax
and National Insurance, so real payroll can differ by a few pounds; it uses the England
and Northern Ireland tax bands. Thresholds (especially student loans) change each April.
To actually pay staff you must run HMRC-recognised payroll software and file RTI - a
spreadsheet cannot do this. Confirm every figure against GOV.UK and with a qualified
accountant or payroll provider before relying on it.
