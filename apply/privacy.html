1. Who this notice is for

This notice is for candidates who register with Haverton Recruitment And Staffing, including through our online registration form, people who apply to our adverts, and referees whose details candidates give us. It explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and your rights under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

3. Where we get it

Mostly from you, including what you enter in our online registration form and the CV you upload. We also receive information from job boards and professional networks you have used to apply, from referees you name, from clients who give feedback, and from official sources such as professional registers and the Home Office online right to work service.

Where we rely on a Schedule 1 condition, we keep an Appropriate Policy Document as the Data Protection Act 2018 requires. We do not make decisions about you using only automated means.

5. Who we share it with

  • Clients who may employ you, only for a role you have agreed we can put you forward for.
  • Referees you have named, to request references.
  • Service providers who help us run our business under contract, including our secure cloud database and CV storage (Supabase, hosted in London, UK), email, file storage and accountancy services. They may only use your data on our instructions.
  • Regulators, the police or other authorities, where the law requires it or to protect someone from harm (for example a safeguarding concern).

We do not sell your information. We aim to keep your information in the UK. If a provider stores data outside the UK, we will make sure there are legal safeguards, such as UK adequacy regulations or the International Data Transfer Agreement, and tell you on request.

7. Your rights

You have the right to: ask for a copy of your information; ask us to correct it; ask us to delete it; ask us to limit how we use it; object to us using it for legitimate interests or marketing; and ask us to transfer it. Some rights have exceptions, for example where the law requires us to keep records. We will reply within one month. To use any right, contact the Director at info@havertoncare.co.uk.

8. Keeping it safe

Access to our systems is limited to people who need it, protected by individual passwords, and recorded. CVs uploaded through our form are stored in private storage that only authorised Haverton staff can open. Sensitive documents such as identity documents are kept in restricted storage, not in our general database.

9. Complaints

Please talk to us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, 0303 123 1113.

10. Changes

We will update this notice when our practices or the law change. The version number and date are at the top.
